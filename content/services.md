<p class="standfirst">End to end. One team, one problem, start to finish.</p>

We don't sell a menu of services. We take a real business problem and stay with it from the first conversation to a product people rely on. Here is what that involves.

<h2 id="find-the-problem"><span class="num">01</span>Find the problem</h2>

Discovery, done properly. We spend time with the people who live with the problem: what it costs, where the workarounds are, what has been tried. We map how things actually work today, not how the org chart says they do. The output is a clear, written statement of the problem worth solving and what "solved" would look like — and sometimes the honest answer that it doesn't need a product at all.

<h2 id="build-the-solution"><span class="num">02</span>Build the solution</h2>

Design and prototyping. We start with the smallest thing that could solve the problem and put a working version in front of real users early. Design, data and engineering are done by the same small team, so decisions are made in days, not handovers. We build on modern, well-supported technology chosen to fit the problem, and we keep the scope tight until the solution is proven.

<h2 id="create-the-product"><span class="num">03</span>Create the product</h2>

From solution to product. Once it works, we turn it into something durable: hardened, documented, secured and measured, with the training and support your people need to own it. Then we keep iterating on what the usage data tells us, so the product grows with the business rather than sitting still after launch.

## Across every stage

<dl class="pairs three">
  <div><dt>Data</dt><dd>We instrument what we build, so decisions are made on evidence rather than opinion.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Technology</dt><dd>We pick tools we'd be happy running for years, and we don't lock you in.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>People</dt><dd>The product only counts as finished when your team is using it without us.</dd></div>
</dl>

## What it's like to work with us

A short, fixed-length discovery comes first, so both sides can walk away cheaply if the problem isn't worth solving. Then we build in short cycles, with a working version at the end of each. The people you meet at the start are the people who do the work throughout.
